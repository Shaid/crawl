**Wizardry 6: Bane of the Cosmic Forge** is the second full documentation
section in Crawl, alongside Black Crypt. It follows the data across three
different releases rather than treating one platform as the universal oracle:
the Amiga port, the DOS/EGA build and the Super Nintendo version.

The research began from a deliberately difficult position: no useful public
format documentation was found for the Amiga corpus. The formats below are
therefore derived from the shipped files, the game's own loaders and targeted
disassembly, with confidence called out as **confirmed**, **rendered** or
**hypothesis** where that distinction matters.

## What is already solved

- The Amiga corpus is structurally accounted for: one `Bane` executable, 60
  `.PIC` files, 12 `.EGA` files, 5 `.HDR` files, 4 `.DBS` files and 35 sound
  files — 117 files in total.
- Amiga `.PIC` cels, `.EGA` screens, `mazedata.ega`, fonts, portraits, headers,
  messages and the major database sections have confirmed readers and render
  or tile with zero-deviation structural checks.
- The Amiga dungeon view is now a real cell-driven renderer: 14 levels decode,
  per-cell wall evaluation is traced, and the shared walker has real corridor,
  door, side-wall and ceiling/floor assets.
- The SNES opening sequence, palettes, dungeon-art libraries, maze records,
  creature bank, spell animations and dungeon-view composer are extracted.
- The DOS/EGA side inherits and independently confirms most of the Amiga
  resource formats, while its endian conversion and overlay architecture are
  documented separately.
