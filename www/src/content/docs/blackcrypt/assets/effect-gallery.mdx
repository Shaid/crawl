# Spell Effects Gallery

Real, frame-accurate playback of the game's own particle simulation for each
of the 95 `BCSPEED.EFF` effects — fireballs, chants, traps, glyph runes and
more — not just the raw 16×16 sprite frames. Names come from the confirmed
effect→spell mapping (92 of 95 attributed); the few still-unattributed
indices are simply not shown, rather than given an invented name. Hover a
card to pause it.
