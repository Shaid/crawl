# Might & Magic — The Secret of the Inner Sanctum

MM1 is treated as a finished ground-truth integration rather than a fresh
format rediscovery. The repo contains the DOS/EGA retail corpus, 55 decoded
maze screens, wall and monster art, starter roster screens, executable tables
and the 55 map-script overlays.
