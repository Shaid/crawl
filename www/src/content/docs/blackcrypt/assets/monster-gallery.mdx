# Monster Gallery

All **204** distinct monster sprites from `bcdfb` through `bcdfn`, grouped by
creature. Each card cycles through that creature's stored size/depth ladder;
hover a card to pause it. The confirmed names come from the game's manual, clue
book, ending text, or DOS archive. The remaining entries retain their
map/creature labels instead of being given invented names.
