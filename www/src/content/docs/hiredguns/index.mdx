description: Recon overview of Hired Guns (DMA Design, Amiga, 1993) — RNC1 layer, 41 maps that each hold a 20×23×23 voxel cube in three planes, a custom figure packer with DMA's own toolchain as the oracle, and the CD32 dev archive's map header.
---

Hired Guns is DMA Design's four-player first-person game for the Amiga
(Psygnosis, 1993). This project has done **first-contact reconnaissance only**
(2026-11-21). The RNC1 layer is confirmed. The map geometry is confirmed from
the executable's own address maths, and the developer's map-editor header
confirms it independently. Cell meanings, graphics and the view are still
hypotheses. No extractor is committed, and no assets are published.

This page summarises
[`docs/hiredguns/amiga/data-structure.md`](https://github.com/Shaid/crawl/blob/main/docs/hiredguns/amiga/data-structure.md).
Where the two disagree, the raw notes win.

> **Pitfall: this is not a stack of 2D floor maps.** Each map is **one
> 23×23×20 voxel cube**, and the engine indexes z directly. A walker that
> imports one 2D grid per floor loses the vertical links (stairs, lifts, open
> space above or below). The repository's walker schema has no slot for those
> links yet.

## Packing (confirmed)

**108 files** under `game/` start with `RNC\x01` (Rob Northen method 1),
including the main executable. `ancient decompress` unpacks all 108 without
error, and each output length equals the header's unpacked-size field.

The files that are not RNC1 are these: the 31 `CharactersGfx/*.gfx` and
`MonstersGfx/*.gfx` files, which use a second, custom packer; the IFF ILBMs; the
`.info` icons; and the dev tools.

The unpacked exe holds the path templates `Maps/xx.map` and
`Styles/Stylexx.gfx`. The game patches the digits at runtime, as Black Crypt
does with its `"bcdf?"` template, so the exe has no literal `01.map` string.

## The map: one voxel cube per level

Each map is a fixed 157,738-byte memory image. All 41 files share the same
layout, down to the byte boundaries of their zero and non-zero spans.

| File offset | Size | Content | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| `+0x0000` | 10 | 5 BE words: `w0`,`w1` vary per map (57,142 / 256,194 …); `w2 = w3 = 10`; `w4` = `0x0100` … `0x0401` (the high byte may be the style number) | hypothesis |
| `+0x000A` | about `0x1AE6` | Briefing/log text records `FB 00 yy 00 xx FC <text>` with `~` padding, plus a 26-word block at `+0x20A` | rendered |
| `+0x22EC`–`+0x65F7` | 17 KB | 28-byte object/event records whose long fields look like offsets into the map image (all below `0x2682A`) | hypothesis |
| `+0x6F40` | 42,320 | **Voxel plane 0**: 20 × 23 × 23 cells × 4 B | **structural-confirmed** |
| `+0x11828` | 42,320 | **Voxel plane 1** (plane 0 + `0xA8E8`) | structural-confirmed |
| `+0x1C110` | 42,320 | **Voxel plane 2** (plane 0 + 2 × `0xA8E8`) | structural-confirmed |

The planes sit 43,240 B (`0xA8E8`) apart: 42,320 B of cells plus 920 B
(10 rows × 92) that are zero in all 41 maps.

### Evidence from the executable

The unpacked exe addresses a cell as **`z·0x844 + y·0x5C + x·4`**: a layer is
2,116 B (23×23×4), a row is 92 B (23×4) and a cell is 4 B. It reaches the other
planes of the same cell with `ADDA.L #$0000A8E8`, which appears **41 times**.
The offsets below are file-relative to the unpacked `Hired Guns` image, with the
HUNK header included.

- `+0x1629C`: `MULS #4,D1 / MULS #$5C,D2 / MULS #$844,D3` and the adds. This is
  a full (x, y, z) → offset computation.
- `+0x783E`, `+0x165C0`, `+0x17382`: `MOVE.B 4(A0),D0 / MULU #$844,D0`,
  followed by `MOVE.B 3(A0)|1(A0) … MULU #$17|#$5C`. An entity record carries
  its z, y and x as bytes.
- `+0x8518`: `ADDA.L #$A8E8,A4 / MOVE.W #$42,D0`. This steps to plane 1 and
  tests `0x42`, the value that fills solid rock in plane 0.

### Evidence from the data

- Reshaping plane 0 as `(20, 23, 23)` gives the highest neighbour agreement of
  every candidate shape: **0.72 / 0.84 / 0.84** per axis, averaged over 41 maps.
- In map 01, each upper layer is a 23×23 square framed by `0x40` on all four
  edges. The lower layers are `0x42` rock with carved cavities.
- On the outer axis, **143,802 of 188,460** open cells (all maps) sit directly
  above or below another open cell.

### The developer's own header agrees

The DMA development archive is the Internet Archive item
`hired-guns-cd-32-dev-files` ("Hired Guns Amiga CD32 Beta and Development
Files", uploaded 2021-07-21, public-domain mark). It is now on disk under
`data/hiredguns/devarchive/`, gitignored. Its `MapEditor/HGmapstructure.h`
defines `MAP_HEIGHT 20`, `MAP_WIDTH 23`, `MAP_DEPTH 23` and `FLOOR_SIZE 529`
(= 23 × 23). It also defines per-cell bitfields for floor, block, water, panel,
explosion, variant and aux. Those constants match the shape derived above from
the retail exe. The archive is the **unreleased CD32 build**, so it is a strong
guide, not proof, for the floppy retail files.

Both archive files match the item's own checksums. The tarball
`HiredGunsCD32_DevFiles.tar.xz` is sha1
`621d17bf34a9d60387dc5f6b529efc4058e05c12`, and the ISO `HiredGunsCD32_Beta.iso`
is sha1 `cc44afbe5b637256bbf3bddbbf53f3a2a0e20131`. The archive holds 301 source
files (288 `.s`, 8 `.i`, 5 `.c`) plus 6 `.h` headers. Only 34 of them are under
`Sources/`; the rest sit in `Graphics/` (175), `Data/` (53), `Takeover/` (24) and
`MapEditor/` (12).

### Cell words (hypothesis)

- **Plane 0**, 4 B: byte 1 is a flag/type byte. `0x42` is solid rock, with
  179,694 such cells where plane 1 = `0x0C`. `0x40` is layer frame or open air,
  `0x00` is empty, and there are low values too (`0x01`–`0x05`, `0x20`, `0x21`,
  `0x43`, `0x4A`). Bytes 0, 2 and 3 carry more fields, possibly object and door
  links.
- **Plane 1**, 4 B: byte 1 takes **53 values**, and `0x0C` is solid interior.
  Around open space it takes a different value on each side of a void: `0x0D`
  west of an opening, `0x0B` east, `0x14` north and `0x04` south. This looks
  like a **per-cell block-shape or graphic index**, choosing which Style figure
  to draw.
- **Plane 2**, 4 B: mostly `0x0000 0F00` or `0`, with sparse other values. It
  may be lighting or a second graphic layer.

### Walker answer 1: wall storage is a new kind, a voxel block with a shape code

Whether a face exists is type-derived, as in Dungeon Master, but in three
dimensions: a block is solid or open. In addition, each block carries an
authored shape/graphic index in plane 1 that picks its artwork. Converting each
z-layer to the per-cell model is mechanical (a face exists where the neighbour is
solid). The vertical links need a z-adjacency plane that the walker schema does
not have yet (the "Hired Guns" row of the
[walker map-format decision](https://github.com/Shaid/crawl/blob/main/docs/walker-map-format-future-decision.md),
addendum dated 2026-11-21, later).

**Confidence:** the geometry is **structural-confirmed** by the exe and the dev
header. The face semantics are a **hypothesis**. Which way z runs (index 0 =
top or bottom) is **not known**.

## Figures and the view

### A custom packer, with ground truth on the disk

Character and monster `.gfx` files have this header:

- `+0`: a word that varies.
- `+2`: a long, the unpacked size. It is `0x3BF6` (15,350) for every character
  and `0x4B66` (19,302) for every monster.
- `+6`: a long, the packed size. It equals the file length in **31/31** files.

A packed stream follows, and it always begins `0x7790`. `ancient` does not
recognise it. The likely packer is DMA's own `C/Crunch`, which ships on the
disk.

`MakeFigure/Filters` is a plain-text **cut list** that turns a
`Characters/<name>` ILBM into its `.gfx`. It gives every figure **4 facings**
(`:Left`, `:Right`, `:Front`, `:Back`) at **4 pre-scaled depth sizes**: 32×64,
32×42, 16×28 and 16×20. It also gives a `:Dead` pose and a `:Face`. Once the
packer is solved, the decoded frames must equal these rectangles cut from the
ILBM, byte for byte.

`MakeFigure/Figure.dump`, `Face.dump`, and the record headers in
`Styles/*.gfx` and `Game/Misc.gfx` appear to share one record shape: a size
long, width/height/plane-count words, then 12-byte frame entries
`w, h, x, y, 0, 0, data-offset` (for example `0010 0014 0018 000E 0000 0000
0D1C`). This is a hypothesis. Each frame entry carries its own geometry, so a
fixed-stride scan would miss records.

### Walker answer 2: pre-scaled pieces blitted into depth slots

**Hypothesis, medium confidence.** Figures exist at exactly four pre-drawn
sizes, which fixes the depth steps at 4. The Style banks hold figure records
that carry their own x/y placement. That is the Black Crypt / Dungeon Master
family (depth-slot composites of pre-drawn pieces), not texture mapping or
vectors. The open part is the third dimension: the renderer may draw blocks
above and below the eye layer (ceiling holes, ledges, stairs) as extra slots.
Settling that needs the view routine traced in the exe.

## Paths tried

| Approach | Result | Why it isn't an answer |
|---|---|---|
| Plane-1 byte 1 as a function of the 4- and 8-neighbour solid mask (bits `0x02`, `0x40`, `== 0x42`) | 0.86 / 0.86 / 0.79 functional | Too low to call derived, and no null control was run, so the value is treated as authored |
| `ancient identify` on the `.gfx` packer | "Unknown or invalid compression format" | Not a known packer. The packer is `C/Crunch` and the unpacker is in the game exe |
