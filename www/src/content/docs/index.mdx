## How to read the status

The labels are intentionally uneven. **Reference** means the project has the
deepest curated documentation and is the standard this site is working toward.
**Active** means the extraction and/or viewer pipeline is substantial but has
known follow-on work. **Extracted** means the important shipped data is decoded
and browsable, while the remaining work is mostly coverage or verification.
**Research** means the format work is real and useful, but a major engine or
palette question still gates the next layer.

The [all-games status board](/status/) is the compact snapshot. Each card then
opens that game's page, with its own open-work list and links back to the raw
`docs/<game>/` notes in the repository. Black Crypt retains the full format and
asset guide; the other sections will grow toward that watermark as their
extractors and renderers mature.

## The wider corpus

The repository also contains an uncurated backlog: the Ishar trilogy and an SSI
Gold Box collection. They are intentionally not presented as active
reverse-engineering projects until they have the same kind of status surface as
the projects above. The eight Amiga-family crawlers that sat in
`data/_unexplored_/` were registered on 2026-11-21 and now have their own
pages; Dungeon Master: Theron's Quest (PC Engine CD) is the one title still
waiting there.
