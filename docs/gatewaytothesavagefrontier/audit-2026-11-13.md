- **Docs say.** `gatewaytothesavagefrontier/amiga/data-structure.md:58-62, 72-74`.
- **Audit found.** Levels 21-26 score 0% on adjacency (e.g. L22 0/194, 0/180). Their S/W planes are
  all zero and doorN is 1 in 236/256 cells, so these are not dungeon grids (likely overland). Level
  21 resolves only `{slot1:18}`.
- **Fix.** Correct the claims and add row `gsf-geo-21-26-encoding`.

### 2. Context: combat icon banks (relevant to wilderness assumptions)

Gateway's `dungcom`/`wildcom`/`randcom` files are 25/34/6 blocks, the same pattern the audit found
in Treasures (24x24 5-plane icons, 368 B/block; randcom with mask, 440 B). They are combat-map icon
banks, not wilderness views; do not read wilderness-rendering meaning into them. See
`docs/treasureofthesavagefrontier/audit-2026-11-13.md`.
